## 新增 / Added

- 设置 › 数据与关于 › 本地数据新增「删除全部便签」：一键清空绳上、归档、草稿和回收站中的全部便签；需二次确认，确认后不可撤销，建议先导出完整备份。删除时会自动关闭所有已打开的便笺窗口，且不会把它们重新保存回来。

- Settings › Data & About › Local data gains "Delete All Notes": one click empties every note on the string, in the archive, in drafts, and in the trash. A confirmation dialog guards the action; it cannot be undone, so consider exporting a full backup first. Open note windows are closed automatically and their content is not saved back.
